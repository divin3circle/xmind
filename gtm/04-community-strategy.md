# Community Strategy

> **A community isn't a Telegram group. It's the group of people who would be devastated if the protocol shut down.**

---

## Core Philosophy

XMind Capital's community strategy is built around a single insight: **we are building for builders and believers, not speculators.** Our community will be defined by shared ownership of the mission — cryptographically accountable AI — not by token price action.

This means we optimise for depth over breadth. 100 deeply-engaged members who understand our architecture are worth more than 10,000 airdrop hunters who will leave at the first sign of market stress.

## Platforms & Channels

### Discord — The Protocol's Living Room
Our Discord is structured across three audiences:

| Category | Channels | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Builders** | `#dev-discussion`, `#sdk-help`, `#mcp-integrations` | Developers building on or integrating with XMind |
| **Investors** | `#vault-performance`, `#strategy-showcase`, `#risk-discussion` | LPs sharing returns and debating strategies |
| **Governance** | `#proposal-discussion`, `#risk-rail-votes`, `#curator-applications` | DAO-level debate and decision making |

Monthly "AI Portfolio Review" calls in Discord voice — the team walks through every major decision the protocol's AI agents made that month, what worked, and what didn't. Transparency is the retention mechanism.

### Mirror.xyz — Our Long-Form Home
All technical deep-dives, post-mortems, and governance proposals are published on Mirror. This establishes XMind as a **thought leader** in the intersection of AI and DeFi, and drives organic search traffic. Each article is collectible — early supporters who mint our articles become verifiable early believers.

### X (Twitter) — Real-Time Pulse
The primary short-form channel for:
- **Live rebalance alerts** ("The XMind AI reshuffled $42K in vault assets in the last cycle. Here's what it did and why 👇")
- **Weekly AI decision summaries** shared as visual threads
- Protocol metrics and TVL milestones

## Ambassador Programme

Launched at the beginning of the public beta (Q2 2026):

**XMind Curators** are community members who:
1. Create and publish strategy templates (risk profiles, target assets, rebalance frequency)
2. Stake XMind Points as a quality bond on their strategy
3. Earn a **2% performance fee override** on all deposits that use their strategy

This aligns incentives perfectly: Curators only earn if their strategy actually performs, creating a marketplace of accountable strategy authors.

**5 founding Curator slots** will be allocated to the most engaged community members who discover the protocol during the hackathon phase.

## Governance & Community Ownership

Starting at DAO launch (Q1 2027), all major risk-related protocol parameters move to community governance:

- **Voted on by token holders**: Maximum allocation per strategy (currently hardcoded at 60%), approved protocol whitelist, performance fee splits.
- **Veto mechanism**: Any token holder can flag a proposed `RiskValidator` change for a 48-hour community review window before it goes live.
- **Transparent execution**: All governance votes and their outcomes are logged permanently on-chain and linked directly in the dashboard's audit log.
