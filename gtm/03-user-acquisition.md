# User Acquisition Strategy

> **We don't chase users — we build infrastructure that makes users come to us.**

---

## Our Target User

| Segment | Description | Pain Point We Solve |
|---|---|---|
| **Sophisticated Retail** | Crypto-native investors managing $5K–$500K | No time to monitor 24/7; losing yield to stale positions |
| **DAO Treasuries** | Protocol treasuries holding idle governance tokens + stablecoins | Need yield on idle capital without full governance vote every trade |
| **DeFi Power Users** | Yield farmers who understand composability | Want automated cross-protocol allocation without building their own bots |

Our initial focus is on **sophisticated retail** as the wedge: they move fast, share loudly, and validate product-market fit clearly.

---

## Acquisition Channels

### ✍️ Content & Developer Education
We write the content that does not exist yet: "How to safely give an AI agent access to a DeFi wallet."
- **Weekly blog series**: "Building Accountable AI Agents on Avalanche" — technical, opinionated, shareable.
- **Chainlink CRE tutorials**: We are among the first teams to push the boundaries of CRE. Publishing our architecture as open-source reference implementations generates organic developer inbound.
- **YouTube explainers**: 3–5 minute visual walkthroughs of a vault rebalance cycle, showing the AI's reasoning live.

### 🤝 Ecosystem Partnerships (Q2–Q3 2026)
Strategic integrations that put us in front of existing audiences:
- **Trader Joe**: Co-marketing "XMind-powered strategies" as a native integration on their liquidity dashboard.
- **Chainlink Labs**: As a CRE reference implementation, pursue co-marketing and joint developer events.
- **DeFi aggregators** (Zapper, Beefy Finance, Yearn-style vaults): Integration listings expose us to users already accustomed to yield delegation.

### 💸 Referral Programme (Q3 2026 Launch)
A two-sided referral loop designed for compounding growth:
- **Depositor referrals**: Existing LPs share a referral link. When a new LP deposits through it, both earn bonus XMind Points.
- **Curator referrals**: Community members who author and publish popular strategy templates earn a percentage of the performance fee generated by their strategy — indefinitely.
- This creates a **flywheel**: more depositors → more fee revenue for curators → more curators → more strategies → more depositors.

### 📣 Influencer & KOL Seeding (Q3 2026)
Target mid-tier DeFi influencers (50K–500K followers) who focus on technical audiences:
- Offer early access and a dedicated vault with initial subsidised strategy fees.
- Provide a ready-made "I let AI manage my DeFi portfolio for 30 days" content brief.
- Budget: $15K for initial KOL seeding round, then measure referral conversion before scaling.
